```

Card: POKEMON WIZARDS OF THE COAST
Set: Skyridge

What price tier does this card belong to?
<|eot_id|><|start_header_id|>assistant<|end_header_id|>
Based on the market analysis:
**Price Tier**: 🌟 SECRET RARE
**Analysis**: Average market price: $600.0k...
<|eot_id|>
```
